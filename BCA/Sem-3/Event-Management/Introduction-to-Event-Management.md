# introduction to Event management
## Meaning & Definition
- **Event**
	- An event can be described as public assembly for celebration, education, marketing or reunion. It offers an alternative approach to promoting a product, services or idea and is used as a powerful tool for launching or marketing them effectively.
- **Definiton**
	- kotler defines "an event as planned occurance that are designed to communicate specific message to target audiences."
## Key Elements of Events**
1. **Event Infrastructure**
	- it includes the essential elements without which an event cannot take place, namely, the core concept, core people, core talent core infrastructure.
	- **Core Concept:** It refers to the basic concept behind an event and includes it's major category. It involves the basic visualization, design, and overall planning of the event.
	- **Core People:** Core people are the main organizers of the event. they actively perform, act or participate to influence the audience to create the desired impact.
	- **Core talent:** It refers to the talent of the core people who perform, act or take part in an event.
	- **Core structure:** It is desirable to create a formal structure. That is, there is a proper or structure in which roles and responsibilities are assigned to different member of the organization.
2. **Target Audience**
	- If refers to the individual who have the primary experience of the event and for whom the event organizer intentionally plans the event.
3. **Media**
	- It acts as a channel to communicate  with the event details to target audience. Media includes prints, electronic transit ,etc.
4. **Venue**
	- The venue is the site at a desired location with required audience  capacity and availability for a particular period.
